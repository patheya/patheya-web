01 · Scope & Key Decisions

Revised Scope

This proposal defines the scope for the ICCC platform. The end-to-end pathway runs register → screen → test → AI risk stratification → clinical review → follow-up — covering camp-based citizen registration, screening and sample collection, lab report ingestion, AI-assisted risk stratification, and Doctor/Oncologist and Specialist-led investigation and treatment.

In Scope

  • Citizen registration — Aadhaar verification, ABHA capture / real-time ABHA creation, ICCC Card printing
  • Camp entry — check-in by ABHA Number and retrieval of the complete personal record
  • Screening — vitals, blood sample and other investigation collection, barcode stickers, consent and data-privacy controls
  • Lab result ingestion — PDF report upload, backend PDF processing, storage of screening results
  • AI risk stratification — overnight batch, Negative / Suspect classification; every citizen notified of the outcome by SMS
  • Citizen navigation by SMS — next action, place, deadline, contact details
  • Specialist and Doctor/Oncologist investigation, recommended tests, treatment line (one-time or recurring)
  • Real-time dashboards for all stakeholders and area-wise analytics
  • User management and access control
🆔

ABHA Number — the Unique Identifier

ABHA is ICCC's unique identifier for every citizen. Citizens without one are registered for ABHA in real time during onboarding.

📄

Lab Reports as PDFs

Testing happens offline at the lab. The Lab Technician uploads the report PDF; the ICCC backend processes it and stores the screening results.

🤖

AI Flags Every Case — Every Citizen Notified

An overnight batch classifies each case as Negative or Suspect. Every citizen is notified of their outcome by SMS; only Suspect cases proceed to clinical review by the Doctor/Oncologist.

⚙️

Rules Configured Directly in Database

Risk rules are agreed between the ICCC Admin and the Doctor/Oncologist and applied via direct database configuration — no rules UI in this scope.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Area-based Screening Teams

Screening Technicians handle both registration and screening in the field, and operate under an assigned Area Admin for their area.

🩺

Doctor/Oncologist — Single Clinical Owner

Doctor and Oncologist responsibilities are combined into one clinical role, supported by Specialists who investigate and refer into the same ABHA-linked record.

02 · Who Uses This

Users & Roles

All internal accounts are provisioned by the ICCC Admin. Citizens interact passively through SMS.

RoleInterfaceResponsibilities
Screening Technician (Area-based)Mobile Onboards citizens via Aadhaar verification, captures ABHA (or registers for one in real time), prints the ICCC Card. Checks in returning citizens at the camp by ABHA Number and retrieves the full personal record. Captures vitals, collects blood samples and other investigation specimens, prints and applies barcode stickers, obtains citizen consent, and records clinic details. Operates under an assigned Area Admin.
Lab TechnicianWeb Processes samples offline at the lab, generates the report, and uploads the PDF to the ICCC App.
ICCC AdminWeb / Database Agrees risk-classification rules with the Doctor/Oncologist and configures them directly in the database. Manages users, roles and access control across the platform.
Doctor/OncologistWeb Reviews Suspect cases with the AI summary, records notes and next action, recommends further investigations, and records the suggested line of treatment (one-time or recurring). Receives referrals from Specialists.
SpecialistWeb Views citizen records by ABHA, uploads external reports, recommends follow-up tests, records observations, and refers the citizen to the Doctor/Oncologist.
Area AdminWeb Oversees Screening Technicians and screening activity in their assigned area(s); views real-time area dashboards and statistics.
Management TeamWeb (read-only) Views real-time management dashboards — total screenings, suspects, and more.

Citizen Onboarding & Screening
MODULE 01

Registration

Onboard a citizen onto the platform with a verified identity and a unique ICCC identifier.

  • Aadhaar-based verification to onboard the citizen
  • Information from Aadhaar plus additional details captured in the app (e.g. ABHA)
  • If no ABHA exists — real-time ABHA registration
  • Print the ICCC Card (if not already issued) and hand it to the citizen
  • ABHA Number is ICCC's unique identifier for the citizen
  • Performed by the Screening Technician
MODULE 02

Entry in Camp

Fast check-in for already-registered citizens.

  • Check-in by the Screening Technician using the ABHA Number
  • Citizen identified instantly from the ABHA Number
  • Complete personal information retrieved — app data, Aadhaar and ABHA
MODULE 03

Screening

Capture current medical status and collect samples with traceable barcodes.

  • Current medical information — BP, height, weight, etc.
  • Blood sample collection and other investigations, as applicable
  • Barcode generated from the citizen's unique identifier (ICCC Number)
  • Barcode stickers printed for all sample collection vessels
  • Consent from the citizen — Aadhaar OTP proposed
  • Data privacy controls
  • Screening Technician records the clinic details along with the citizen's record
Lab & Risk Stratification
MODULE 04

Lab Tests & Processing of Results

Offline testing, with digital ingestion of the resulting report.

  • Offline processing and report generation at the lab
  • Lab Technician uploads the report PDF to the ICCC App
  • PDF processing in the ICCC App backend
  • Screening results stored against the citizen's record
MODULE 05

AI Risk Stratification

Overnight batch classifies every case and prepares an explainable summary for the Doctor/Oncologist.

  • Rules agreed between ICCC Admin and Doctor/Oncologist; configured directly in the database (no UI)
  • Classification: Negative or Suspect
  • Every citizen — Negative or Suspect — is notified by SMS
  • Only Suspect cases proceed to clinical review by the Doctor/Oncologist, who records notes and next action
AI Classification (overnight)→ Doctor/Oncologist Review (Suspect only)→ Citizen notified (SMS — every outcome)
MODULE 06

Citizen Navigation

Tell the citizen exactly what to do next.

  • SMS with next course of action, place, deadline and contact details
  • Citizen carries out the action — e.g. further lab tests
Clinical Follow-up
MODULE 07

Further Investigation by Specialists

Specialists work from the citizen's ABHA-linked record.

  • Access the ICCC App and view the citizen's records by ABHA
  • Optionally upload reports from tests done at labs outside the ICCC network
  • Recommend further / follow-up lab tests
  • Reports uploaded to ICCC by the lab, or physical copies handed to the Specialist
  • Feed observations into the ICCC App
  • Refer the citizen to the Doctor/Oncologist
  • Citizen notified by SMS — next action, place, deadline, contact
MODULE 08

Further Investigation by Doctor/Oncologist

Confirm the diagnosis and define the line of treatment.

  • Access the ICCC App and view records by ABHA
  • Optionally upload external reports
  • Recommend further investigations; results uploaded by the lab or handed over physically
  • Feed the suggested line of treatment — one-time or recurring
MODULE 09

Medical Treatment Details

Longitudinal record of ongoing care.

  • Time-to-time entry of recurring treatment details in the ICCC App by the Doctor/Oncologist
Oversight & Administration
MODULE 10

Real-time Dashboard

Live operational visibility for every stakeholder.

  • Real-time statistics — total screenings, suspects, etc.
  • Dashboards scoped by role — Area Admin (their area), Management Team and ICCC Admin (organisation-wide), Doctor/Oncologist (their caseload)
MODULE 11

Analytics

Data vital insights tailored to each stakeholder.

  • Area Admin: screening coverage vs. target per ward/area; Suspect-rate trend over time
  • ICCC Admin / Management: end-to-end funnel — registrations → screenings → suspects → confirmed cases; lab report turnaround time (TAT) compliance
  • Doctor/Oncologist: active caseload and pending reviews; referral-to-treatment conversion rate
MODULE 12

User Management & Access Control

  • Managed by the ICCC Admin — role-based access for every user type
